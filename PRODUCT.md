# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are readers who want to understand monta3135's technical work, interests, and profile. They arrive from the home page, shared articles, search results, or direct links and should be able to identify the author, find relevant writing, and read it comfortably on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

monta's blog is a personal site for publishing concise technical notes and introducing monta3135's work and interests. Success means readers can reach the author's first-hand writing directly, understand the site's structure, and read articles without distraction.

## Positioning

An author-owned, non-commercial record that combines monta3135's engineering notes, current interests, and personal profile in one place. It prioritizes direct, first-hand technical writing over promotion, audience conversion, or platform-driven presentation.

## Operating Context

- Articles are written as Markdown or MDX content and published as a static Astro site on GitHub Pages.
- Readers browse the profile, article index, individual posts, and category pages or subscribe through RSS.
- Shared articles use canonical URLs and generated Open Graph images.
- The site is read in Japanese across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the Astro, Bun, and GitHub Pages workflow.
- Preserve existing public URLs and published article content unless a task explicitly changes them.
- Support article metadata, categories, publication and update dates, RSS, sitemap, Open Graph metadata, syntax-highlighted code, footnotes, and KaTeX-rendered mathematics.
- Keep the site static: there are no visitor accounts, comments, or server-side application workflows.
- Do not introduce advertising, conversion funnels, or fabricated product claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: `monta's blog`.
- Voice: quiet, personal, technical, concise, and deliberate.
- The site should feel authored rather than templated, with the writing and author remaining central.
- Preserve established names, factual copy, routes, locally bundled assets, and the existing author identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Published technical article content in `src/content/blog/`.
- Author profile, interests, and external links in `src/pages/index.astro`.
- Existing article imagery, author imagery, logos, web badges, and generated Open Graph assets in `src/assets/` and the relevant page components.
- RSS, sitemap, category pages, article metadata, and canonical URLs generated by the current Astro implementation.
- No testimonials, audience-size claims, client list, press coverage, or commercial performance evidence is established; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. **Publish first-hand truth.** Present the author's real work, interests, and technical observations without invented claims.
2. **Keep content primary.** Help readers identify, find, and understand the writing with minimal friction.
3. **Remain portable and durable.** Preserve stable URLs, static output, and the established Astro/Bun/GitHub Pages workflow.
4. **Respect the reader.** Avoid advertising, engagement traps, and unnecessary interaction around the articles.
5. **Preserve authorship.** Keep the site's personal voice, factual identity, and source materials intact across future changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline: WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve semantic structure, meaningful image alternative text, keyboard-visible focus states, readable contrast, browser zoom and text scaling, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior for decorative animation.
