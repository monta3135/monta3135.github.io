# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers who want to understand monta3135's technical work, interests, and profile through a quiet personal blog. They arrive from the home page, shared posts, or direct links and should be able to scan the profile and move to articles without friction.

## Product Purpose

monta's blog is a personal technical site for introducing monta3135 and publishing concise engineering notes. Success means the visitor can identify the author quickly, understand the site's structure, and read posts comfortably on desktop or mobile.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, personal, technical, and deliberate. The site should feel authored rather than templated: restrained typography, clear hierarchy, and small visual details that support the content.

Three-word personality: **quiet, technical, personal**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards, marketing funnels, and conversion-first copy.
- Purple gradients, neon accents, excessive glassmorphism, and decorative motion.
- Repeated nested cards or ornamental UI that competes with the writing.
- Tiny low-contrast text, ambiguous navigation, and layout shifts.

## Design Principles

1. **Content leads.** The author's profile and writing remain the primary visual focus.
2. **Quiet contrast.** Use a warm neutral surface and a restrained blue accent; reserve the cat background for atmosphere.
3. **Readable by default.** Preserve clear type scale, line height, focus states, and responsive spacing.
4. **Motion earns its place.** The Dithered Object background is decorative and must never block interaction or reduce legibility.
5. **Preserve existing identity.** Refinements should keep the current copy, routes, fonts, and Astro/Bun workflow intact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline: WCAG 2.1 AA. Keep semantic headings, meaningful image alt text, keyboard-visible focus states, readable contrast over the cat background, and reduced-motion behavior for decorative animation.
